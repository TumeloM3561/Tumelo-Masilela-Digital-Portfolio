# Agile Team Charter

## Project Purpose
Tech Empower Labs aims to support schools with computer labs, ICT equipment, networking, maintenance, and technical support.

An Agile Team Charter defines the team's purpose, roles, responsibilities, working agreements, and communication methods. It helps team members understand what is expected of them and how they should work together to achieve the project's objectives.

For Tech Empower Labs, the team charter establishes how members contribute to the project, coordinate their activities, share information, and handle challenges. Having clearly defined responsibilities helps reduce confusion, improve accountability, and support effective teamwork.

## Reflection
The Agile Team Charter was the starting point for our Tech Empower Labs project. It set out our shared purpose, our roles and responsibilities, and the working agreements we would follow. Writing it showed me that a team needs to agree on how it will work before it begins the actual work. Without that agreement, each member would have worked from their own assumptions about who does what and how often we communicate.

As Product Owner and Team Leader, I learned that leadership in an Agile team is less about giving instructions and more about keeping everyone aligned. The charter gave us something to point back to when questions came up about responsibilities or expectations. It also made each team member’s role visible, which helped everyone understand how their contribution fitted into the bigger picture of providing digital learning support to schools.

One challenge was balancing a clear structure with flexibility. A charter that is too rigid can feel like a set of rules, while one that is too vague does not help anyone. I learned that a good charter is a living agreement that the team can revisit and adjust as the project develops.

In future projects, I would review the charter with the team at the end of each sprint instead of treating it as a one-time document. I would also encourage every member to speak up if a working agreement is not working for them. The charter also connects to the rest of my portfolio. The roles we defined here shaped how tasks were assigned on our Trello board and how we reported progress in the logbook.
