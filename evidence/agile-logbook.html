My reflection

Maintaining the Agile logbook helped me understand the importance of recording daily progress, identifying outstanding tasks and communicating with team members. The entries helped us keep track of completed work and prepare for upcoming activities.

I learned that recording blockers is important because it helps the team identify issues that may delay progress. For example, waiting for all members to join the Trello board was a challenge noted during the walkthrough.

In future projects, I would improve my planning and reporting by updating the logbook consistently, recording clearer next steps and reviewing task progress with the team.
