My reflection

The Agile Team Charter helped our Tech Empower Labs team establish a shared understanding of our project goals, responsibilities, and ways of working. The project focused on providing ICT support and computer lab solutions to schools.

Through this activity, I learned the importance of communication, teamwork, and clearly defined responsibilities. I also learned that team members need to understand their roles and work together to achieve the project objectives.

One lesson I took from this activity is that agreeing on responsibilities early can help reduce confusion and improve collaboration. In future projects, I would encourage regular communication and review our team agreements as the project develops.
